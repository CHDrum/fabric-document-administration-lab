# 06 - Wrap Up and Handoff

**Duration:** 5 minutes

**Objective:** leave an accountable demonstration and a realistic enterprise next-step list.

## Steps

1. Record your learner workspace, pipeline, Warehouse, model, and report links. Note which SQL/model/report/access checks passed and which remain open.
2. Explain the sequence to a partner: catalog keys and revision history first, invoice disposition and job-grain reconciliation second, shared measures and sponsor analysis third.
3. Name the seven enterprise subjects: catalog/mastering and physical/digital inventory; communication preferences/constraints; constituents; access/delivery history; cost/financial reporting; operational/process/audit/regulatory/mandate/vendor reporting; lifecycle/archival reporting. The core lab implements only selected catalog, job, and cost slices.
4. Capture production discovery items: source owners, gateway/connectors, extraction cadence, source performance, finance reconciliation, retention, privacy, support, and EDW partnership. The small team should reuse acquisition/transformation/publication contracts rather than duplicate report-specific logic.
5. Save work and verify no pipeline is running. Keep the learner workspace only as long as your organization's workshop policy permits. Remove only your explicitly designated disposable learner workspace when directed by its owner. **Never pause, resize, or delete shared capacity, and retain the instructor demonstrations.**

## Challenge and Answer

**Question:** What makes this more than a new report silo?

**Answer:** Explicit source contracts, separate grains, reusable published products, shared measures, quality evidence, and named owners create an integration surface for the EDW team. Enterprise reuse is still a partnership and governance task, not something the synthetic demo proves automatically.

## Completion Checklist

- [ ] Links and verification status handed to the facilitator.
- [ ] No schedules, unintended sharing, real records or credentials left behind.
- [ ] Savings targets remain labeled as targets.
- [ ] Ready for the 20-minute break and [834 lab](https://github.com/CHDrum/fabric-834-analysis-lab).
